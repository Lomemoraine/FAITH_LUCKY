🌸 What is TFL SafeSpace? (The Big Picture)

TFL SafeSpace is a confidential, culturally safe digital mental health platform built specifically for Kenyan youth and young adults. It eliminates the fear of stigma by allowing anyone to share their struggles 100% anonymously, listen to real audio stories, access licensed tele-psychologists, and receive immediate crisis safety net intervention when in acute distress.

🕊️

1. Anonymous Peer Rooms

Youth post and reply without real names or emails. Generates soothing handles like @GentleSeeker304 and avatars with full voice recording support.

🧠

2. AI Semantic Crisis Triage

Powered by OpenRouter & Gemini AI to understand subtle cries for help across English, Swahili, and Kenyan Sheng (e.g. farewell notes or giving away items).

🛍️

3. Care Store & Care Passes

Supporters buy affirmative merchandise (Hoodies, Journals) via M-Pesa. Every purchase unlocks free Care Pass counseling vouchers for youth in need.

📱

4. Android SMS Gateway

Uses a local Android phone with a Safaricom SIM to send instant crisis alerts to on-duty counselors and deliver Care Pass voucher codes directly to users' SMS inboxes.

💡 Quick Note for Testers: You do not need to write code or run commands. All testing can be done using your normal web browser and mobile phone by following the step-by-step guides on the next pages.

🧪 How to Test Each Component (Step-by-Step)

Follow these friendly, click-by-click instructions to test each part of the platform.

1
Anonymous Community & Voice Stories

Testing anonymous posting and audio playback

Peer Support

Goal: Verify that someone can share what they are going through anonymously without revealing identity.

  • Open the app at http://localhost:3002 (or your live web link).
  • Click on any Listening Room (e.g., Anxiety & Panic, Grief & Healing, or Burnout & Career).
  • Notice your anonymous handle at the top (e.g., @QuietSoul795) and peaceful lotus/sun avatar.
  • Type a safe personal reflection in the share box: "Today was stressful at school, but taking slow deep breaths is helping me get through it."
  • (Optional) Click the microphone icon 🎙️ to record a quick 5-second voice note.
  • Click "Share Gently".
✅
What You Should See: Your post appears instantly in the room feed under your anonymous handle. If you recorded audio, an interactive soothing audio waveform player appears that anyone can play.
2
AI Semantic Crisis Triage & Instant Help

Testing Kenyan Swahili/Sheng crisis detection & phone SMS alert

Safety Net

Goal: Verify that when a user expresses acute distress, the system flags it, offers emergency helplines, and alerts the on-duty counselor.

  • In any Listening Room, type a message expressing acute hopelessness in Sheng, Swahili, or English: "Hii safari ya dunia nimefika mwisho nawaachia kila kitu sitakuwa hapa kesho" (Or in English: "I gave away all my belongings to my sister, this is my final night")
  • Click "Share Gently".
✅
What You Should See:
  • An emergency crisis modal immediately opens displaying Kenya 24/7 free hotlines (Befrienders Kenya 0722 178 177, Red Cross 1199, LVCT 1190).
  • The post is quietly flagged and sent to the on-duty psychologist's moderation desk.
  • The on-duty counselor's physical phone automatically receives an urgent SMS alert!
3
Care Store & M-Pesa Voucher Delivery

Testing merchandise purchase & automated SMS Care Pass delivery

Merch & Pass

Goal: Test how buying physical merchandise funds counseling sessions and texts a Care Pass code to the buyer.

  • Click on the Care Store tab in the navigation.
  • Browse products like the TFL Signature Hope Hoodie (KES 2,800) or Guided Healing Journal (KES 1,200).
  • Click "Support & Unlock Care Pass" on any item.
  • Enter your Kenyan phone number (e.g. 0712345678 or 0105505631).
  • Click "Complete M-Pesa Checkout".
✅
What You Should See: A success receipt appears on screen with your generated Care Pass code (e.g. CARE-HOPE-TFL). Simultaneously, your Android gateway phone automatically sends a real SMS text message containing the voucher code directly to your phone number!
4
1-on-1 Confidential Psychologist Consultation

Testing voucher redemption and live private counseling chat

Tele-Therapy

Goal: Test how an anonymous client redeems their Care Pass code to start a private session with a licensed counselor.

  • Click on the 1-on-1 Counseling tab.
  • Choose a verified licensed Kenyan psychologist (e.g., Dr. Faith Mwangi, Licensed Clinical Psychologist).
  • Enter your voucher code (e.g. CARE-HOPE-TFL or test code CARE-DEMO-TFL).
  • Click "Redeem & Start Confidential Session".
✅
What You Should See: A private, end-to-end encrypted session room opens. You receive a welcoming message from Dr. Faith Mwangi and can exchange confidential messages in real-time without revealing your real name.
5
Clinical Moderation Desk (/moderation)

Testing 1-click clinical de-escalation & private crisis room invite

Clinician Portal

Goal: Verify how clinicians review flagged posts and intervene with verified badges and 1-on-1 room invites.

  • In your browser, visit http://localhost:3002/moderation.
  • See all active flagged cases color-coded by severity (CRITICAL in rose, PRIORITY in amber).
  • Click "💙 Send Verified Clinical Response & Invite to Private Room".
  • Pick an empathetic starter chip (e.g. "You are heard and safe here. Please take a slow breath...").
  • Ensure [x] Open & link confidential 1-on-1 private crisis room is checked.
  • Click "Post Clinical Reply & Resolve".
✅
What You Should See: The case is instantly resolved in the moderation queue, and a verified clinical badge response (🩺 [TFL Verified Clinical Team — Dr. Faith Mwangi]) is published with a private crisis room link for the user.

Keyword What the Phone Automatically Does

VOUCHER <CODE> Queries the database and texts back if your Care Pass code is valid, active, or redeemed.
